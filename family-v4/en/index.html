Getting here ↗Ride availability ↗
Do I need to choose every ride in advance?

No. Save what you like or try “Decide at the park”. Saving a ride does not automatically add it to your order.

Can I buy for the whole family at once?

Yes. The demo group planner considers ticket holders, height restrictions and adult accompaniment. A bundle can be shared, while each person gets a separate unlimited pass.

Where will my tickets appear after payment?

Right on screen. You can save them and find them later in this browser’s purchase history. Email is optional. This version issues sample tickets only.

What if the payment result is unknown?

Check the status of your saved order. Do not pay again. If only some tickets are ready, those samples remain available.

What else is at the park?

Dino Park, ZooZoo Land and places to eat. Find the details in the visit section.
